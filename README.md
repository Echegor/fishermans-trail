# Fisherman's Trail & Lisbon Master Expedition

An interactive, high-resolution editorial presentation and expedition guide for trekking Portugal's **Fisherman's Trail** (*Trilho dos Pescadores* / Rota Vicentina) from Porto Covo to Aljezur, followed by an executive transfer and retreat in Lisbon.

## Live Presentation
🔗 **[View Presentation](https://echegor.github.io/fishermans-trail/)**

## Features
- **5 Complete Coastal Stages**: Detailed trail conditions, elevation profiles, water deserts, and safety warnings from Porto Covo down through Aljezur (93 km).
- **Interactive Transit Route Switchers**: Dynamic comparison of private Mercedes chauffeur transfers, express coaches, rental cars, and train logistics for both Lisbon arrival and departure.
- **Interactive Expedition Route Atlas**: Stage inspector with live GPS waypoint links, distance/elevation summaries, and boutique lodging overviews.
- **Curated Retreats & Gastronomy**: Full breakdowns of Pestana Porto Covo Village, Monte do Zambujeiro, Herdade do Touril, Casas do Moinho, and AlmaLusa Baixa/Chiado in Lisbon.
- **Dynamic Theme Engine**: Toggle between *Porcelain & Slate*, *Atlantic Navy*, and *Obsidian & Gold* luxury palettes.
